Free AI Toolkit

The AI features you can use today on free web chats: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. Nothing here needs a paid plan or tokens.

Which feature do I need?

1 · Context: attach or paste your evidence

Free: ClaudeFree: ChatGPTFree: GeminiFree: Copilot

An AI only knows what you give it, plus what it learned in training. Give it your sources and tell it to stay inside them. That one habit prevents most wrong answers.

Use only the evidence below. Do not invent policies, deadlines, button labels, or menu names. If something is missing or uncertain, write "Verification note" instead of guessing.

[EVIDENCE]
Try it: run the weak prompt first, then this one. Compare.

2 · Saved instructions: say it once

Claude: Projects (where shown)ChatGPT: custom instructionsGemini: Gems (where shown)Copilot: text file

Each tool lets you save standing instructions. Names and free availability change, so check the day before. A plain text file you paste works everywhere.

You are a writing collaborator. Follow this voice in every reply.
- Address the reader as "you". Use active voice and present tense.
- Lead with the answer. No preamble, no recap.
- Keep sentences under 25 words.
- Never use: simply, just, easily, obviously, seamless, leverage.
- If you are unsure of a detail, write "TODO: confirm" instead of guessing.
Try it: save this, open a new chat, and paste exercise_pack/paste_in_examples/weak_page.md. Ask for a review.

3 · Side panel: edit the draft, not the chat

Claude: ArtifactsChatGPT: CanvasGemini: Canvas

Ask the AI to put the draft in the side panel. You then change one section without regenerating everything, which also saves your free message limit.

Put the revised page in a side panel (artifact or canvas) so I can edit it. Change only the section I name next.

4 · Images: show it, don't describe it

ClaudeChatGPTGeminiCopilot

All four accept images on free plans, with daily limits. Use this to check a screenshot against your steps. The AI reads what it sees. It cannot know what the live product looks like today.

I am attaching a screenshot and the steps that refer to it. For each step, quote the label you see in the screenshot. Flag any difference from the step. If you cannot read something, say "not visible". Do not guess.

5 · Roles and a second opinion

All four tools

One chat that writes and checks its own work tends to agree with itself. Use a different role, a fresh chat, or a different AI to verify.

LevelHow
GoodAsk for a skeptical-verifier role in the same chat.
BetterOpen a fresh chat. Paste only the draft and the evidence.
BestPaste the draft into a different AI tool.
Act as a skeptical verifier. Check the page against the evidence. Return a table: claim | source evidence | status (supported / unsupported / needs verification) | fix. Do not rewrite yet.

6 · Web search: use on purpose

Limited on free plans

Search helps with public facts. It cannot see your private policy. In this workshop, turn it off or tell the AI not to use it, so answers come from your evidence. Search and research modes also burn free limits fastest.

Do not search the web. Use only the evidence I pasted.

7 · Voice and memory

Voice: phone appsMemory: varies

Voice: dictate a prompt or have a reply read aloud. Useful on a phone. Memory: some tools remember details across chats. For work that must be repeatable, rely on a saved instruction you can see, not on memory.

8 · Trust: the habit that matters most

  1. Limit the sources.
  2. Make the AI mark what it is unsure of.
  3. Check claims with a second pair of eyes, human or AI.
  4. Confirm anything that changes fast (labels, menus, numbers) against the live product.
  5. A human approves before publishing.

What comes after free

Everything above is manual: you paste and click. Paid and developer tools can automate these same habits: run checks on every save, connect to your tools, and update pages on a schedule.

The workshop does not need any of it. A demo and a take-home guide are in examples/README.md. The original Claude Code guide is in _archive_aperture/index_claude_code.html.
